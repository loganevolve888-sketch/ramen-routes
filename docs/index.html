            How SlurpQuest works

            Logging a stop

            Search a place in the map toolbar, tap the map, or press N anywhere. Rate the broth, the noodles and the vibe, and the bowl becomes a passport stamp with a pin on your world map.

            Keyboard shortcuts

            • N Log a new noodle stop
            • / Search your stamps
            • M Search for a place on the map
            • ? Open this help
            • Esc Close any dialog

            Your data

            Stamps live only in this browser’s local storage — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. Clearing site data wipes your passport, so use Export passport in the footer to keep a backup, and Import backup to restore or move to another device.

            The only thing that leaves your browser is chat messages and place searches (sent to OpenStreetMap’s free geocoder).
